💡 Why Move India's Notification Architecture Wins in Smart India Hackathon:
  • Zero Data Audio Synthesis: Chimes are computed in real time mathematically via the browser's audio DAC using sine/sawtooth oscillators. Zero `.mp3` downloads, zero latency, works 100% offline.
  • Multi-Channel Dispatch: Combines audio alarms, smartphone haptic vibrations, system notifications, and in-app visual heads-up displays.
  • Dual-User Persona: Commuters receive boarding proximity alerts; City Police & Municipal authorities receive real-time infrastructure and law enforcement emergency feeds.
